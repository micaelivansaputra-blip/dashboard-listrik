    How to read this page

    Variance to budget(Actual kWh − Budget kWh) ÷ Budget kWh. Plus means more electricity than planned, shown in red. Minus means less, shown in green. Within 2% counts as on budget.
    Change vs another monthCompares average kWh per day, so a 31-day month and a 30-day month are fair to each other.
    Zero Occupancy sitesSeven cinemas in the energy control program. Four started on 19 August; three more were added on 9 October.

    Data notes

    • Actual kWh comes from the corrected invoice file. September kWh for Kalibata City Square, Q Mall Banjarbaru, Mall of Serang, Palembang Icon, Citimall Sampit and Java Supermal use the corrected invoices.
    • Paragon Square Mall is left out of July totals and of any comparison with July, because its first invoice covered a partial period.
    • K-Square Batam is still ramping up, so it sits far below budget in all three months.
    • Month-to-month changes use average kWh per day (July 31 days, August 31 days, September 30 days).
    • Invoice periods can start mid-month, so a billed month may differ from the calendar month.
